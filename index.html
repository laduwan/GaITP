Takes one to know one

A premier, compassionate practice.

We provide talk therapy in its truest form — addressing anxiety, stress, depression, grief, trauma, and more with a rare balance of humor and genuine care. A proud LGBTQ+ ally, committed to inclusive, affirming support for every client.

Led by a licensed team — LPC, NCC, CPCS, BC-TMH — serving clients across Georgia, Florida, Idaho, and Texas.
