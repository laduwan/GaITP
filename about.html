About

Kejuiana Johnson, LPC, NCC, CPCS, BC-TMH

Founder of GA Integrated Therapeutic Perspectives LLC.

Photo coming soon

I started GA Integrated Therapeutic Perspectives to build the kind of practice I’d want to walk into myself — direct, unhurried, and genuinely tailored to the person in the room, not a script.

My work centers on evidence-based approaches — CBT, DBT, and Solution-Focused Therapy — applied to anxiety, depression, grief, and moderate trauma. I also work with individuals navigating life-changing diagnoses, including spinal cord injury and infertility, and I hold an LGBTQ+ affirming, culturally inclusive space for every client who walks through the (virtual or physical) door.

I hold active licensure in Georgia, Florida, Idaho, and Texas, and I’m a Certified Professional Counselor Supervisor (CPCS), which means I also supervise counselors working toward full licensure.
