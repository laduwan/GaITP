What we offer

Therapy tailored to what you’re carrying.

Individual and couples sessions, available virtually across GA, FL, ID & TX, or in-office in Hinesville — plus clinical supervision for counselors working toward licensure.

Individual therapy

Anxiety, Depression & Grief

One-on-one sessions using CBT, DBT, and Solution-Focused Therapy to work through anxiety, depression, grief, and moderate trauma at a pace that fits your life.

Couples therapy

Relationship Support

Practical support for couples navigating conflict, communication breakdowns, or major relationship transitions.

Life transitions

Major Life Changes

Support for the identity and logistical upheaval of big transitions — career shifts, moves, loss, and everything between.

Specialized care

Life-Changing Diagnoses

Focused support for individuals adjusting to a new diagnosis — MS, diabetes, spinal cord injury, or infertility — and the emotional weight that comes with it.

Clinical supervision

Supervision for Counselors

As a Certified Professional Counselor Supervisor (CPCS), I provide clinical supervision for counselors working toward full licensure.

Affirming care

Inclusive by Design

LGBTQ+ affirming and culturally inclusive — every client is met as they are.

How sessions work

Virtual or in-office.

Virtual Sessions

Secure telehealth for clients in Georgia, Florida, Idaho, and Texas, backed by BC-TMH certification.

In-Office Sessions

In-person sessions at the Hinesville, GA office for local clients who prefer face-to-face care.

Insurance Accepted

Major insurance accepted — Aetna, Cigna, BCBS, Humana, Tricare, Optum, and Magellan.
