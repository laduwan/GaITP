Services

Therapy tailored to what you’re carrying.

Individual and couples sessions, available virtually across GA, FL, ID & TX, or in-office in Hinesville.

Individual Therapy

Anxiety, Depression & Grief

One-on-one sessions using CBT, DBT, and Solution-Focused Therapy to work through anxiety, depression, grief, and moderate trauma at a pace that fits your life.

Couples Therapy

Relationship Support

Structured, practical support for couples navigating conflict, communication breakdowns, or major relationship transitions.

Life Transitions

Major Life Changes

Support for the identity and logistical upheaval that comes with big transitions — career shifts, moves, loss, and everything in between.

Specialized Care

New & Life-Changing Diagnoses

Focused support for individuals adjusting to a new diagnosis — including spinal cord injury and infertility — and the emotional weight that comes with it.

How sessions work

Virtual or in-office — your call.

Virtual Sessions

Secure telehealth sessions available to clients in Georgia, Florida, Idaho, and Texas, backed by BC-TMH certification.

In-Office Sessions

In-person sessions available at the Hinesville, GA office for local clients who prefer face-to-face care.

An Affirming Space

LGBTQ+ affirming and culturally inclusive by design — every client is met as they are.
